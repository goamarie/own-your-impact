# Own Your Impact

A free kit to remember, prove, and keep the good work you've done, with a Claude agent that keeps it growing every week.

Built for knowledge workers, not just engineers: a recall exercise, a weekly collector agent in your work Claude, and two records: a detailed Internal Impact Record that stays in approved work systems, and a Personal Career Record of plain Markdown files you own and can use with any AI.

## What's here

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | Landing page (deployed on Netlify) |
| `Own-Your-Impact-Guide.pdf` | The full step-by-step guide |
| `My-Career-Brain.zip` | Starter folder: structure, templates, and every prompt |
| `thanks.html` | Feedback form confirmation page |

## Start here

1. Download `My-Career-Brain.zip` and unzip it into your personal cloud.
2. Open `Own-Your-Impact-Guide.pdf` and follow the Quick start table.
3. Curious about who owns your professional memory? See Food for Thought at the end of the guide, or `food-for-thought.md` in the starter folder.

## Ground rules

Your ideas, skills, experience, and record of what you accomplished are yours. Company-owned material stays. Follow your employment agreement and company policies. Describe it, don't copy it. This is general information, not legal advice.
